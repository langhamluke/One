# Teen-Owned Banking & Investing Accounts: User Reviews (Step, Fidelity Youth, Schwab Teen, Chase First, Capital One MONEY)

> Method note (2026-10-01): The research environment's egress proxy blocked direct fetches of apps.apple.com, play.google.com, trustpilot.com, thecollegeinvestor.com and joingerald.com. All figures below come from search-engine snippets of those pages and of third-party reviews, so they could not be checked against the live page. Treat the ratings and counts as approximate, dated "as indexed". Reddit threads could not be reached directly either. Reddit sentiment is cited through secondary sources that quote it.

## Q1. Current ratings, review counts, and complaint themes (App Store, Google Play, Trustpilot, BBB, CFPB)

### Takeaway
Both Step and Fidelity Youth have high app-store averages (about 4.7–4.8). The low-volume review venues (Trustpilot, BBB, WalletHub) are much more negative. For Step, those negative reviews center on account limits and closures, frozen funds and support available only by chat or email. No product-specific CFPB data could be retrieved.

### Cited Findings
**Step**
- App Store: 4.8/5 according to one review site. Others cite 4.7/5 on about 82K ratings. Exact current figures are unverified. — [Young and the Invested](https://youngandtheinvested.com/step-banking-card-review/); [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/step-borrow-build-credit/id1438006983)
- The app is now marketed as "Step: All-In-One Money App". Its earlier names included "Step – Teen Banking", "Step: Bank and Build Credit" and "Borrow & Build Credit", which shows a repositioning beyond teens toward young adults and borrowing. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/step-all-in-one-money-app/id1438006983); [JustUseApp (old name slugs)](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1438006983/step-teen-banking/reviews)
- Trustpilot: 4/5 from 1,281 reviews, as of Aug 4, 2026 (search snippet). — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/step.com)
- BBB: B- rating, 11 complaints, 1 unresolved. The search also surfaced an F-rated "Step" BBB profile with an investigation note dated Jan 8, 2026 (BBB contacted the company about complaint patterns and got no response). It is unclear whether that is the same Step Mobile entity, so verify before citing. — [BBB Step profile](https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/palo-alto/profile/financial-services/step-1216-1531244); [BBB complaints](https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/palo-alto/profile/financial-services/step-1216-1531244/complaints)

Step says remaining funds were returned to the original funding accounts by bank transfer. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/step.com); [BBB](https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/palo-alto/profile/financial-services/step-1216-1531244/complaints)
- No phone support, only in-app chat or email. Users describe "generic canned responses" and say customer service is "non-existent". — [BBB / review aggregation](https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/palo-alto/profile/financial-services/step-1216-1531244/complaints); [MyBankTracker](https://www.mybanktracker.com/checking/reviews/step-card-teen-banking-account-review-432126)

### Inferences
- There is a large gap between app-store averages (about 4.7–4.8, likely helped by in-app rating prompts to satisfied users) and complaint venues. The pain is concentrated in a minority of users who hit a compliance or fraud review, but for them it is severe (money is locked).

## Q2. Step: loved features, hated features, and 2025–2026 changes

### Takeaway
Users love that Step is a no-fee card that builds credit for under-18s; it is described as the only secured card available to minors. They also like the rewards and savings rate. They hate frozen or closed accounts, chat-only support and withdrawal limits. The product has moved from a "teen bank" toward a general young-adult money app with a paid tier (Step Black), cash advances (EarlyPay) and even mobile phone plans.

### Cited Findings
**Loved**
- It is "the only secured credit card available for users under age 18" and reports to all three bureaus with no interest. Step's own survey says 18-year-olds who used Step for 7+ months averaged a 725 credit score (company-sourced, so not independent). — [Young and the Invested](https://youngandtheinvested.com/step-banking-card-review/); [Step families page](https://step.com/families)
- No-fee basic account and Visa card. — [Wellkept Wallet](https://wellkeptwallet.com/step-card-review/)

**Step Black (paid or earned tier)**
- Step Black costs $4.99/month and is waived with $500+/month in direct deposit. It offers up to 8% back at partners, 3% on dining, food delivery and donations, 2% on entertainment, gaming and streaming, and 1% on everything else. — [The College Investor](https://thecollegeinvestor.com/35336/step-review/); [Step help center](https://help.step.com/en/articles/9324271-how-does-the-step-black-monthly-subscription-work)
- Step's site now calls Step Black "an earned status available at no cost" and advertises a savings reward "over 7x the national average". — [Step.com](https://step.com/)
- Savings rate history: 5.00% on up to $250K launched May 2023, later 5.00% up to $1M for Step Black, and 3.00% now shown on step.com/save. That points to a rate cut in 2024–2025 (exact date unverified). — [BusinessWire, May 2023](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230517005139/en/Step-Launches-5.00-on-Savings-Balances-up-to-$250000); [Step Save page](https://step.com/save)

**Newer products, signaling repositioning**
- Step EarlyPay (up to $500) and Step Mobile Plans ("unlimited data, talk, and text plans starting at just $15/month"). — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/step-instant-cash-and-rewards/id1438006983)
- In 2022 Step announced a crypto and stock investing product, building on its survey finding that 56% of Step teens were "bullish on crypto". — [BusinessWire, Apr 2022](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220412005546/en/Step-Announces-Plans-for-a-New-Crypto-Stock-Investment-Product)

**Hated**
- Account limits, closures and frozen funds, including two months unable to withdraw, spend or deposit after a "suspicious activity" flag. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/step.com); [MyBankTracker](https://www.mybanktracker.com/checking/reviews/step-card-teen-banking-account-review-432126)
- Declined withdrawals because of a cap of 6 withdrawals per 30 days, plus low withdrawal amounts to external banks. — [MyBankTracker / JustUseApp snippets](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1438006983/step-teen-banking/reviews)
- Support by chat or email only, with canned replies. — [BBB](https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/palo-alto/profile/financial-services/step-1216-1531244/complaints)

### Inferences
- Moving Step Black from a paid add-on to an "earned status" tied to direct deposit means teens without paychecks may not reach the best rewards and rate. Feature changes like this are a likely source of "they changed it" complaints.
- The app's rebranding toward EarlyPay and phone plans suggests Step is chasing monetizable young adults. Teen-specific parent features may get less attention.

### Gaps
- No exact dated 2025–2026 changelog was found for Step Black's terms or the savings-rate cut.
- No dated Reddit (r/CreditCards, r/povertyfinance) Step threads were retrieved.

## Q3. Fidelity Youth: fractional shares, debit card, education, parental oversight, transition at 18, limits, usability

### Takeaway
Reviewers praise the structure: the teen owns the account, it has no fees or minimums, and it offers fractional US stocks, ETFs and Fidelity mutual funds plus a debit card. Execution draws complaints: a buggy app, weak parent visibility and dropped rewards. Major 2026 news: the standalone Fidelity Youth app is reportedly being folded into the main Fidelity Investments app, though sources conflict.

### Cited Findings
- Teen-owned (ages 13–17) brokerage account. The parent has oversight but is not an owner, so it is neither custodial nor joint. No fees, no minimums, $0 commissions on US stocks and ETFs. — [Fidelity overview](https://www.fidelity.com/go/youth-account/overview); [The College Investor](https://thecollegeinvestor.com/79875/fidelity-youth-account-review/)
- Teens can trade most US stocks, ETFs and Fidelity mutual funds, including fractional shares. No options or margin. The free debit card has no domestic ATM fees. — [Fidelity FAQs](https://www.fidelity.com/go/youth-account/faqs); [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/money/buying-stocks/articles/fidelity-to-offer-no-fee-brokerage-accounts-to-teenagers)
- A parent must already hold a Fidelity account. — [joingerald (snippet)](https://joingerald.com/learn/saving--investing/fidelity-youth-account-review-guide)
- At 18 the account can convert to a standard brokerage account, adding options and margin eligibility. The parent then loses monitoring and closing rights. — [BusinessWire launch](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210518005725/en/); [Fidelity overview](https://www.fidelity.com/go/youth-account/overview)
- Compared with Schwab: Fidelity's parent has "very limited control", while Schwab's joint account gives the parent full joint control. Fidelity has an automatic money market sweep and Schwab does not. — [CNBC Select](https://www.cnbc.com/select/fidelity-youth-vs-schwab-teen-investor-account/)
- Rewards changes: Fidelity removed the 5-cent-per-debit-swipe reward. One source says "Learn and Earn" was being removed, while another says Learn and Earn rewards remain, alongside a $50 reward for opening and activating a new account. — [Google Play review snippet](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fidelity.teen&hl=en_US&gl=US); [proverbs27flocks, Apr 27 2026](https://proverbs27flocks.com/2026/04/27/fidelity-youth-account-changes-and-educating-teens/); [wealthvieu](https://wealthvieu.com/investing/fidelity/fidelity-youth-account/)
- App consolidation: "the separate Fidelity Youth app … is being discontinued", with features moving into the flagship Fidelity Investments app as a "tailored experience" for teens. The pitch is a smoother handoff at 18 because the teen keeps the same app. — [proverbs27flocks, Apr 27 2026](https://proverbs27flocks.com/2026/04/27/fidelity-youth-account-changes-and-educating-teens/); [wealthvieu](https://wealthvieu.com/investing/fidelity/fidelity-youth-account/). *Conflict:* other 2026 snippets still describe the standalone app as active ([joingerald](https://joingerald.com/learn/saving--investing/fidelity-youth-account-review-guide)). Verify on fidelity.com.
- Usability: Reddit parents in Dec 2024 called the app "half-baked", "horrible" and "unusable" next to Greenlight, citing bugs and missing features (reported second-hand, not independently verified). — [joingerald](https://joingerald.com/learn/saving--investing/fidelity-youth-account-review-guide); [ScreenWise](https://screenwiseapp.com/media/fidelity-youth-app)
- Fidelity's 2026 awards ranked it #1 for Youth Investors (StockBrokers.com). — [StockBrokers.com](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/fidelityinvestments)
- A first-person teen account of starting to invest through Fidelity Youth exists (not read in full). — [Substack](https://amateurentrepreneur.substack.com/p/how-i-got-into-investing-fidelity)

### Inferences
- The likely user split: experts and parents love the account, while the app has historically been rated weaker by actual users. Folding it into the main app could fix bugs and the transition at 18, but teens might lose the simpler, teen-styled interface.
- Removing small gamified rewards (5 cents per swipe, possibly Learn and Earn) takes away the "savings motivation" hooks teens respond to.

### Gaps
- Official Fidelity announcement and date for the app sunset (fetch blocked; conflicting secondary sources).
- Direct teen quotes on the education content quality; none were retrieved.

## Q4. What teens want that these lack, plus comparables (Schwab, Chase First, Capital One MONEY)

### Takeaway
Teens and Gen Z want real interest on savings, real investing (including crypto), credit building and autonomy. No single product delivers all of them. Step covers credit building, Fidelity and Schwab cover investing, and the bank accounts (Chase, Capital One) cover basic spending with parent control but pay little or no interest.

### Cited Findings
- 77% of Gen Z would save more if they earned interest. 44% of savers know their rate, and 46% of savers have under $500 saved. — [Cash App press (teen savings 3.5% APY)](https://cash.app/press/savings-interest-for-teens)
- 68% of Gen Z are interested in investing in stocks and 55% in crypto. — [CNBC Select](https://www.cnbc.com/select/gen-z-prioritizes-investing-over-traditional-financial-milestones/)
- In a Step survey, 56% of Step teens were bullish on crypto and 25% called investing the most important skill to learn before graduating. — [BusinessWire, 2022](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220412005546/en/Step-Announces-Plans-for-a-New-Crypto-Stock-Investment-Product)
- Fidelity's 2023 study found teens think investing is important but fewer than 1 in 4 have started. — [BusinessWire, Sep 2023](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230906913084/en/Fidelity-Study-Reveals-Teens-Think-Investing-Is-Important-but-Fewer-Than-1-in-4-Have-Actually-Started)
- Gen Z expect financial independence at an average age of 32. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/gen-zers-aim-financially-independent-111500881.html)
- **Schwab Teen Investor**: a joint account (parent has full control) for ages 13–17. Stocks, ETFs, mutual funds and fractional shares, $0 fees, and a course that pays $50 in fractional shares if finished within 45 days. — [CNBC Select](https://www.cnbc.com/select/fidelity-youth-vs-schwab-teen-investor-account/); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/7-things-to-know-about-schwabs-new-brokerage-account-for-teenagers-142336241.html)
- **Chase First Banking**: ages 6–17, parent co-owner, requires a Chase checking account. Offers chores and allowance tracking and savings goals. No APY. Only one parent can manage the account. — [CNBC Select](https://www.cnbc.com/select/chase-first-banking-account-review/); [Finder](https://www.finder.com/banking/chase-bank/chase-first-banking)
- **Capital One MONEY**: ages 8–17, no fees, 0.10% APY, separate parent and teen logins. Complaints: a second parent cannot be added, a "known issue" with transfers into the account (blog, Sep 2023), and no category- or merchant-level spending limits. — [Finder](https://www.finder.com/banking/capital-one-bank/capital-one-money-teen-checking-account); [Jed Fonner blog, 2023-09-02](https://jedfonner.com/posts/2023-09-02-CapitalOne-MONEY)

### Inferences (synthesis; not directly sourced)
- **Missing across products:** (1) a teen account that combines investing with credit building; (2) a competitive savings rate on teen bank accounts (0–0.10% at Chase and Capital One versus the 3%+ teens respond to); (3) crypto access for under-18s (none of the listed products offer it); (4) multi-parent access (Chase and Capital One); (5) savings motivation and gamified rewards, which Fidelity is pulling back on; (6) responsive human support (Step); (7) a clear, staged path to independence. Fidelity's teen-owned model is the closest to that path, and Schwab's joint model is the furthest.
- No sources surfaced teen demand for social features or tax help. Treat those as unvalidated hypotheses.

### Gaps
- No direct r/teenagers or r/personalfinance threads with dated teen quotes were retrieved (Reddit not reachable).
- No current (2026) ratings or complaint data for Schwab Teen Investor or Capital One MONEY.
- No evidence was found on teens wanting tax-filing help or social or peer features from these apps.

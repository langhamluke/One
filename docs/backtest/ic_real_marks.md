_Generated by `engine/letf/ic_replay.py` from `data/fixtures/0dte_real/` (offline). Sessions 2026-09-14 to 2026-10-08._

## Bottom line

* **The bot as written would not have traded on any of the 17 sessions with real option data.** Every non-event session failed the $0.30 minimum-credit rule (A entries: 0). The trend filter never fired (largest 09:30-09:45 move was 2.23 against a limit of about 3.43).
* **Real mid credit at the bot's strikes was $0.03-$0.14 (median $0.05)** per share for the 5-wide put / 3-wide call condor with shorts at +/-(VIX/16)% of spot. That is far below the $0.30 floor, so variants A and B have n = 0.
* Why (5-minute sessions, where ATM legs were also pulled): at 09:45 the 0DTE ATM straddle implied a 1-sigma move to the close of about $3.05-$4.62, while VIX/16 gave $6.87-$8.27. The short strikes sit about 1.7-2.5 sigma out in the 0DTE market's own terms, where each option is worth a few cents. VIX (30-day) is the wrong ruler for a 6-hour option; VIX1D, which the bot prefers, is not served by the connector.
* Even the naked short put + short call at half the distance (+/-0.5 x exp_move) were worth only $0.27-$0.84 combined at 09:45, before paying for any wings.
* Forcing a trade every non-event day (variant C) gives n = 17, total $-96.44 per contract, average $-5.67: the $0.02 + $0.02 fill concessions and $0.32 fees are of the same size as the entire credit. The 25% target is worth 0.25-3 cents per share, less than the $0.02 exit concession, so a target hit still loses money unless the debit gaps far below the target between checks. Holding to 15:45 (C2) loses less only because more legs decay to $0.00-$0.01.
* **n is small (17 sessions, 7 with exact 5-minute timing, 10 with 10-minute approximate timing) and cannot establish or rule out an edge.** This is a sanity check of credit size and fill realism against a modeled backtest, nothing more.

Sessions 2026-09-14 to 2026-09-28 therefore use 10-minute bars (see the timing note below).
* SPY 5-minute bars cover 2026-09-14 to 2026-10-08 (no interpolated bars). VIX 09:45 values are the close of the 13:40Z 5-minute bar.
* Interpolated bars on the four condor legs: 2026-10-08 (lp 10:35, sc 12:00, lc 10:35 ET bar ends). None of them was on an entry, check or exit bar actually used, so no substitution was needed.
* Option bars are trade/mark prices, not bid/ask. The first bar of each session comes back with open = high = low = close; only closes from 09:45 on are used.

## Assumptions

* **Vol source:** VIX (VIX1D not available from the Robinhood connector), 5-minute bar 13:40Z close = 09:45 ET. The VIX1D >= 25 skip is therefore inactive, as in the bot's own fallback path. exp_move = spot x (VIX/16)/100.
* **Spot / open:** spot = close of the SPY 13:40Z bar (09:45 ET); open = open of the 13:30Z bar.
* **Strikes:** sp = floor(spot - exp_move), sc = ceil(spot + exp_move) on the $1 grid, lp = sp - 5, lc = sc + 3. Every strike was confirmed to exist with `get_option_instruments` (state=expired).
* **Entry:** mid credit = sp + sc - lp - lc from leg closes at the first bar end at or after 09:45; fill = credit - $0.02 (the bot's limit). Always assumed filled.
* **Management:** at every bar end until 10:20 ET, debit = sp + sc - lp - lc; exit when debit <= fill x 0.75; otherwise exit at the 10:20 mark. Exit fill = debit + $0.02. The live bot polls every 60 s; bar closes are coarser, so hits between bar ends are missed.
* **Fees:** $0.04 per leg per side, $0.32 per condor round trip (rough Alpaca regulatory/OCC pass-through; Alpaca charges no options commission). P&L per contract = (fill - exit fill) x 100 - fees.
* **10-minute sessions (09-14 to 09-28):** entry marks are the 09:50 ET bar close (5 minutes after the decision), checks run at 10:00 / 10:10 / 10:20, and the hold-to-15:45 exit uses the 15:40 close. The table at the end reruns the 5-minute sessions on this 10-minute grid to show how much the coarser timing moves the numbers.
* **Fill realism:** a 2026-10-07 SPY chain snapshot (`data/fixtures/SPY_chain_20261007.csv`) shows a $0.01 bid/ask on every SPY option marked under $0.20. Taking the natural price on four legs costs about 4 x $0.005 = $0.02, so mid - $0.02 is roughly natural, provided the bar close is close to mid. At 1-cent ticks a 1-tick error per leg is 20-100% of these credits, so the P&L below is accurate only to a few dollars per contract.
* **Event days:** the bot's list (2026-10-02, 2026-10-14, 2026-10-15, 2026-10-28, 2026-10-30, 2026-11-06); 2026-09-16 FOMC added for September. No option data was pulled for event days.

## Caveats

* Small n: a handful of calm days (VIX 14-17) in one regime. Nothing here estimates tail risk. No short strike was breached in the window (closest: 2026-09-21, SPY high 774.89 vs the 775 short call, close +0.93 x exp_move).
* Marks are bar closes of trades/marks, not quotes; several deep-OTM legs print the same $0.01-$0.03 for hours, which may be stale last-trade values rather than live mids.
* The fill model assumes every limit fills (mid - $0.02 in, mid + $0.02 out).
* To extend: add `legs_YYYYMMDD.csv` (5-minute) or `legs10_YYYYMMDD.csv` files, VIX rows and SPY bars for new sessions and re-run the script. 10-minute bars reach back further than 5-minute bars, so a larger 10-minute sample is available on request.
